Who we are

Hexo TD: Idle Tower Defense ("the app", "we", "us") is an indie mobile game developed by HexGrid Games. If you have any questions about this policy, see the contact section at the bottom.

Information we collect

Information you provide

Hexo TD does not ask you to create an account or enter your name, email address, or any other personal information. There is no text chat or other way to send messages to other players.

Game progress data

Your progress, upgrades, in-game currency balances, game statistics and settings are saved locally on your device. We do not receive a copy of this save data, and it is not backed up to our servers.

Leaderboard

When you open the leaderboard, the app submits your Combat Power score along with a random player ID and an automatic display name (for example, "Knight" followed by four characters from your player ID). These are created by the app and are not based on your name or email address. Your display name and score are visible to other players.

Data collected automatically via third-party SDKs

The app includes third-party SDKs for advertising and gameplay analytics. These SDKs may automatically collect certain device and usage information, as described below, under the provider's own privacy policy. We use gameplay analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it.

Advertising ID & personalised ads

The app uses your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID) to serve ads. Ads may be personalised based on your interests. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Open Settings on your Android device
  • Go to Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads on some devices)
  • Tap "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" or "Delete advertising ID"

Opting out means you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests. It does not affect your ability to play the game.

Children's privacy

Hexo TD is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and that data about them should be removed, please contact us using the details below and we will address it promptly.

Data retention & deletion

Your local game save is removed from your device when you uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not remove your leaderboard entry or analytics data held by Unity.

To ask us to delete your leaderboard entry and player data, email us with your leaderboard display name, shown in the app's leaderboard. For data collected by Unity's advertising services, please refer to Unity's privacy policy for information on its retention practices and how to submit a deletion request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable privacy law, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. To make a request about your leaderboard or analytics data, contact us using the details below. Requests about advertising data should be directed to Unity.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you may have additional rights. Please contact us if you have questions about exercising these rights.
